# Welcome to HelmutOrg

HelmutOrg is the home of **Helmut.jl** and its related tools, documentation, and
research software. We bring together the code and resources supporting the
development of Helmut and its scientific community.

## Helmut.jl

Helmut.jl is a modular snow model written in Julia for exploring the physical
processes that shape snow cover. It combines a finite-volume solver with
processes such as heat diffusion, compaction, and snow metamorphism, and supports
flexible boundary conditions and external forcing.

## Funding and acknowledgements

Helmut is developed within the **IVORI** project (*New Insights on the Snow
Cover: From Snowflakes to Ice Sheets, in Seconds to Centuries*).

IVORI is funded by the **European Research Council (ERC)** under the
**European Union's Horizon 2020 research and innovation programme**, through
an ERC Starting Grant (grant agreement **949516**), and is coordinated by
**Météo-France**.

Learn more about the project and its funding in the
[official IVORI project record](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/949516).
